## Grantsville

The city Planning & Zoning source page at `https://grantsvilleut.gov/departments/planning___zoning/index.php` returned `403 Forbidden` in the browser on 2026-05-15, so PDF enumeration is proceeding through indexed city document-center links and direct PDF verification. Initial public PDF/map leads found in search results include the Grantsville Future Land Use Map city-center PDF, the Proposed Future Annexation Expansion Map PDF, municipal-code-hosted general plan ordinances, and city packet PDFs where general plan map amendments are embedded. Direct PDF verification is still required before classification.

The public Grantsville zoning map page was extractable via stateless page extraction and listed 8 map documents under **Zoning Maps**: three Future Land Use sectional maps (City Center, Deseret Peak, Flux), Proposed Future Annexation Expansion Map, three current zoning maps (Central, Deseret Peak, Flux), and Sensitive Areas Boundaries Map. Opening the City Center direct PDF in the browser confirmed it is a single-page map PDF/image titled `FUTURE_LANDUSE_MAP_JAN2020_CITY_CENTER`.
